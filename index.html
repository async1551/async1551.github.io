The team behind the project

Club 19 is a group of young people striving to make a big change. We believe that when young people come together with respect and honesty for the common good, they can change the world for the better.

However, why?

On our server, we have a few rules that keep us united and in good vibes. We gather tools, tips and knowledge to make the lives of persistent people smoother and less chaotic.

Server Laws

  • Respect to others: Treat all members with respect and kindness. No form of harassment, discrimination or offensive behaviour will be accepted.
  • Inappropriate Content: Do not share or discuss content that is explicitly NSFW or offensive. This includes text, speech, images, videos or links.
  • Online Threats: All forms of threats, including doxxing (disclosure of personal information without consent), are strictly prohibited.
  • Illegal Activities: Discussing or promoting illegal activities is not allowed.
  • Accounts: Only one account is allowed within the community. (Alt accounts)
  • Charity: All forms of hatred, including, but not limited to, racism, sexism or discrimination are strictly prohibited.
  • Trolling: No discriminatory attempts of a provocative or annoying nature, known as trolling to an annoying point towards another person.
  • Content: Do not post spam, advertisements or forward servers.
  • Self-Promotion: Self-promotion is prevented on all channels
  • Moderation: Please follow the instructions of the moderation team.
  • Disrespect: Do not intentionally disrupt the community or the Discord server.
