The team behind the project

Club 19 is a group of young people striving to make a big change. We believe that when young people come together with respect and honesty for the common good, they can change the world for the better.

However, why?

On our server, we have a few rules that keep us united and in good vibes. We gather tools, tips and knowledge to make the lives of persistent people smoother and less chaotic.

Server Rules

  • Respect to Others: Treat all members with respect and kindness.
  • Inappropriate Content: No NSFW or offensive content.
  • Online Threats: Strictly prohibited.
  • Illegal Activities: Forbidden.
  • Accounts: Only one account per user.
  • No hatred or discrimination.
  • No trolling behavior.
  • Stay on topic and avoid spam.
  • Media must align with guidelines.
  • Respect privacy.
  • Follow admin instructions.
  • Violations may result in punishment.
  • Appeals via DMs.
