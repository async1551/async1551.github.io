Club 19

Let's be the change we want to see on the internet.

The team behind the project;

Club 19 is a group of young people striving to make a big change. We believe that when young people come together with respect and honesty for the common good, they can change the world for the better.

However, why?

On our server, we have a few rules that we don't mess around with — that is, basic principles that keep us united and in good vibes. We believe that progress comes when we help each other and share, not when we play competitive games (no rage quits). . We gather tools, tips, and knowledge from here and there to make the lives of persistent people smoother and less... 🤡 chaotic. Everything that goes up here, we want to be useful and worth your time — not just to fill up the chat.

Server Rules

  • Respect to Others: Treat all members with respect and kindness. No form of harassment, discrimination, or offensive behavior will be tolerated.
  • Inappropriate Content: Do not share or discuss any content that is explicitly NSFW or offensive, including text, speech, images, videos, or links.
  • Online Threats: All forms of threats, including doxxing (sharing personal information without consent), are strictly prohibited.
  • Illegal Activities: Discussing or promoting illegal activities is forbidden.
  • Accounts: Only one account per user is allowed within the community (no alternate accounts).
  • Hatred & Discrimination: All forms of hatred, including racism, sexism, or any discrimination, are strictly prohibited.
  • Trolling: No provocative or annoying behavior intended to provoke others (trolling) will be tolerated.
  • Chat: Stay on topic and avoid spamming.
  • Media Sharing: Only share content that aligns with the server's guidelines and rules.
  • Respect Privacy: Avoid sharing personal or sensitive information without explicit consent.
  • Violations of these rules may result in warnings, muting, or bans.
  • Repeated offenses will lead to permanent bans.
  • The moderation team reserves the right to act in the best interest of the community.
